# Google Ads History Diagnosis Scope

Included:

- read-only historical exports;
- search terms;
- keywords;
- landing pages;
- campaign daily performance;
- local CSV outputs only;
- sanitized documentation.

Excluded:

- campaign edits;
- budget or bid changes;
- keyword or negative keyword changes;
- ad changes;
- conversion setup changes;
- CRM exports;
- credentials or secrets.
