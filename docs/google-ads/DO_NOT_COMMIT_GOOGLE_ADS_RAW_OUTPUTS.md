# Do Not Commit Google Ads Raw Outputs

Never commit:

- raw CSV or TSV exports;
- `google-ads.yaml`;
- OAuth JSON;
- refresh tokens;
- access tokens;
- developer tokens;
- full customer IDs;
- screenshots with account data;
- lead or CRM personal data.

Use local outputs only under `automation/google-ads-readonly/output/` or another local non-synced folder.
